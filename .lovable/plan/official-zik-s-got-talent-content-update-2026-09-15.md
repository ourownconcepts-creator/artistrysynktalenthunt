# Official ZIK’S GOT TALENT content update

## Goal
Make every public-facing page clearly present **ZIK’S GOT TALENT 1.0** as a University of Ibadan campus-wide talent competition, organized by Zik Hall Royals in collaboration with the Zik Hall Executive Council, under the theme **Where Talent Meets Opportunity**.

## Current wording conflicts found
- The homepage and sitewide descriptions call the event a **national** competition rather than a University of Ibadan campus-wide competition.
- The homepage leads with a generic discovery line instead of the official event name, edition and tagline.
- The About page positions the event as part of the ArtistrySynk ecosystem and does not name the organizer, collaborator or institution.
- Several pages expose internal operational language such as “admin-configurable,” “configured independently,” and technical privacy/auditing details instead of audience-focused event messaging.
- ArtistrySynk is sometimes presented too prominently or as a permanent-profile entitlement, rather than as the supporting creative identity and networking layer.
- Sponsor copy describes configuration instead of the value of supporting student talent and the University of Ibadan community.
- Public metadata repeatedly uses the old mixed-case name and generic or inaccurate descriptions.
- Public journey copy does not follow the official discovery-to-opportunity story.

## Content changes

### 1. Establish the official identity sitewide
- Standardize public display copy to **ZIK’S GOT TALENT** and use **ZIK’S GOT TALENT 1.0** where the edition is relevant.
- Replace “national competition” claims with “University of Ibadan campus-wide talent competition.”
- Name **Zik Hall Royals**, **Zik Hall Executive Council**, and **University of Ibadan** in the About page and footer description.
- Preserve compact labels where full capitalization would reduce readability, while keeping the canonical brand presentation in headings and descriptions.

### 2. Rewrite the homepage message
- Lead with **ZIK’S GOT TALENT 1.0** and **Where Talent Meets Opportunity**.
- Use the short official description: a campus-wide competition created to discover, showcase and celebrate exceptional student talent.
- Refine category, competition-journey and closing-action headings around “Discover. Showcase. Celebrate.” and “Your stage starts here.”
- Remove unsupported national scope and unrelated category examples.
- Keep all live dates, counts, statuses, rounds, sponsor records and buttons functioning exactly as they do now.

### 3. Strengthen About and event-purpose content
- Replace the existing About introduction with the supplied long-form identity in a natural, concise format.
- Explain the organizer/collaborator relationship clearly.
- Present the five objectives: hidden-talent discovery, creativity, healthy competition, youth empowerment and student unity.
- Reframe the ArtistrySynk section as a supporting partnership, not the organizer or owner of the event.

### 4. Clarify participation and supporting pages
- Rewrite the How It Works journey around: create creative identity, choose category, register, audition/showcase, compete, and step into opportunity.
- Refine public copy on Categories, Competitions, Contestants, Track Entry, Announcements, Voting and Registration pages so it is student-focused and avoids internal admin language.
- Keep actual steps, fields, rules, judging, voting and registration behavior unchanged.
- Leave database-driven category names, requirements, rules and descriptions unchanged, because the request explicitly forbids database changes. Surrounding page copy will use the official category list without presenting unconfirmed requirements.

### 5. Correct ArtistrySynk positioning
- Use: **“ZIK’S GOT TALENT discovers the talent. ArtistrySynk connects the talent.”**
- Explain ArtistrySynk as the digital creative platform supporting onboarding, creative identity, wider networking and opportunities beyond the competition.
- Remove wording that makes ArtistrySynk appear to organize the event or guarantees an unsupported entitlement.
- Keep the existing identity connection flow and every integration message that affects user actions intact.

### 6. Rewrite sponsor, footer and contact messaging
- Replace technical sponsor-page copy with partnership value around talent discovery, youth development, creativity, student engagement and brand visibility.
- Update footer copy with the official organizer, collaborator and institution.
- Add only the confirmed email and social handles where suitable in existing public content; do not invent contact channels or sponsorship packages.

### 7. Align search and social-preview text
- Set the homepage title to **ZIK’S GOT TALENT 1.0 | University of Ibadan Talent Competition**.
- Use the supplied homepage meta description and social-sharing description.
- Create concise, page-specific title and description variations for each public route using natural phrases such as “University of Ibadan talent competition,” without keyword stuffing.
- Preserve existing metadata structure and make no route, domain, canonical, image or publishing changes.

## Technical boundaries
- Change text values only in existing public page and shared-copy files.
- No database, authentication, registration logic, APIs, routes, integrations, components, design, layout or architecture changes.
- No invented event facts, criteria, requirements, sponsorship prices or benefits.
- Dynamic content supplied by the database remains unchanged.
